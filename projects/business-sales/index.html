What’s on the file.

Shown publicly

Sector, asking price band, real estate inclusion (included, lease in place, or optional), deal structure (asset, share, or either), general geography, years in operation, and the vendor’s post-close involvement profile.

Available on engagement

Trading name, exact address, vendor identity, financials and tax returns, lease summary, customer and supplier concentration, employee details, and the listing brokerage’s full deal book. Released after a signed non-disclosure agreement and a qualification call.

Business listings shown are available through cooperating brokerages unless otherwise stated. HomeLife G1 Realty Inc., Brokerage represents the buyer's interest in any transaction unless otherwise disclosed in writing. Where a business is sold together with its underlying real estate, only the real estate component of the transaction is conducted under TRESA through the brokerage. The sale of business assets, shares, or goodwill is conducted by the parties' respective counsel and is not a real estate trade. Listing details, asking prices, and inclusions are provided by the listing brokerage or vendor and are subject to change without notice. Any specific representation requires a written buyer representation agreement and, where applicable, a non-disclosure agreement before financials are released.
