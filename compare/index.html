About — opening glyph & rules

How to read this. Each column renders the same elements at the same scale on the same navy background. The only thing that changes column-to-column is the gold hex value. Background #0A0D14 matches the live site. Type is Fraunces (display italic) and Inter (labels), same as production.

Where to look. The brand mark at small scale (the right-hand "P" in the first row) and the eyebrow rule + lane dashes (rows 3 and 7) are the toughest tests. If the gold dies on those, it will die on the live site too. The About glyph (last row) is the most theatrical use and shows where the gold sits emotionally — quiet/refined or weighty/decisive.
