Category 01

Off-Market Acquisitions.

Buyer representation on Ontario real estate that never reaches the open market.

A meaningful share of Ontario residential and multi-unit transactions closes without reaching MLS. Power of sale files, private sales between principals, assignment purchases, and estate sales are sourced brokerage-to-brokerage.

Primegate represents investors and cash-ready buyers on the acquisition side of these files. Matching is by documented acquisition criteria.

How the process works

i.

Documented acquisition criteria at intake

Geography, asset type, budget, closing timeline, financing pathway. Captured at the outset so matching is intentional, not opportunistic.

ii.

Matching against files as they surface

Qualified buyers are notified when a property matches their documented criteria. Multiple representation, where applicable, is disclosed and consented to in writing by both parties before any showing.

iii.

Structuring, offer, closing

Handled through Primegate under HomeLife G1 Realty Inc., Brokerage. Your real estate lawyer stays in the loop from the outset.
