Category 02

Income & Multi-Unit Property.

Buyer representation on Ontario income and multi-unit acquisitions where the rent roll, legal-use, and financing decide the outcome.

Income property is won at the rent roll and the legal-use confirmation, not at the listing photo. The value sits in what the tenants will confirm in writing, whether the claimed secondary suite is legitimately permitted, and whether the cash flow holds against realistic carrying cost.

Primegate represents investors on the acquisition side of income and multi-unit files across Ontario. Matching is by documented investment criteria: geography, asset type, budget, yield focus, and financing pathway.

What we source

01Duplex through small apartment buildings
02Legal secondary and garden-suite properties
03Mixed-use residential over commercial
04Small commercial income (retail, office, light industrial)
05Cottage and recreational income holdings
06Condo investment holdings

How the process works

i.

Documented investment criteria at intake

Geography, asset type, budget range, yield focus, and financing pathway. Captured at the outset so matching is deliberate, not opportunistic.

ii.

Rent roll, legal-use, and financing diligence before offer

Rent roll verification against what the tenants will confirm. Legal-use confirmation for claimed secondary suites, garden suites, and short-term rentals. Residential Tenancies Act exposure on residential files. Financing structure worked through before the number gets agreed.

iii.

Structuring, offer, closing

Handled through Primegate under HomeLife G1 Realty Inc., Brokerage. Your real estate lawyer handles the estoppel review and closing conveyancing. Multiple representation, where applicable, is disclosed and consented to in writing by both parties before any material step.
